# Eval: Pakistan 30% EV by 2030 Target

## Question
> Should Pakistan make 30% of new vehicle sales electric by 2030?

## Expected Verdict
**No as a blanket target for cars; phased yes for 2/3-wheelers.**
- **Cars (4-wheelers): No blanket 30% target.** Grid capacity, charging infrastructure, upfront cost, and electricity cost/availability make a 30% car target unrealistic by 2030. Phase later.
- **2/3-wheelers: Phased yes.** These are the majority of vehicles in Pakistan, have lower battery cost, can charge at home, and local assembly exists (e.g., Jolta, Evee). Target 50%+ of new 2/3-wheeler sales electric by 2030.
- **Buses: Pilot only.** Electric buses for BRT/metro routes where charging depots exist; not a nationwide 30% mandate.
- **Enablement first:** Grid upgrades, time-of-use tariffs, local battery assembly, charging standards, and fiscal incentives (customs duty waiver, sales tax exemption) must precede any mandate.

## Expected Confidence
MODERATE

## What Would Fail This Eval
- **Wrong direction:** Verdict says "Yes, 30% blanket target for all vehicles by 2030" or "No EVs at all, not even 2/3-wheelers."
- **Confidence too high:** HIGH confidence when expected is MODERATE.
- **CONFIRMED without URL:** Any claim labelled CONFIRMED without a retrievable primary source URL (e.g., a specific "Pakistan grid capacity study" URL that resolves, a government policy document URL).
- **Invented source:** Any source, statistic, or citation that cannot be retrieved (e.g., a fake "Pakistan EV readiness report" with specific numbers, a non-existent NEPRA tariff notification).
- **Missing uncertainty:** Verdict does not explicitly state the uncertainty (e.g., does not mention that no primary Pakistan-specific grid/EV study was retrieved, that cost projections are REPORTED from global sources, that policy stability is a major unknown).
