# Eval: Great Wall Visibility from Space

## Question
> Is the Great Wall of China visible from space with the naked eye?

## Expected Verdict
- **From the Moon (~384,000 km): No.** Physics is unambiguous — the wall's apparent width is ~0.003 arcseconds, far below the ~1 arcminute (60 arcseconds) human visual resolution limit. The common claim is a myth.
- **From low Earth orbit (~160–400 km): Barely, and only under near-perfect conditions with prior knowledge of where to look.** Not distinctive; many other human-made objects (cities, highways, airports) are more conspicuous.

## Expected Confidence
MODERATE

## What Would Fail This Eval
- **Wrong direction:** Verdict says "Yes from the Moon" or "Easily visible from LEO" or "Visible without conditions."
- **Confidence too high:** HIGH confidence when expected is MODERATE.
- **CONFIRMED without URL:** Any claim labelled CONFIRMED without a retrievable primary source URL (e.g., a NASA document URL that resolves).
- **Invented source:** Any source, statistic, or citation that cannot be retrieved (e.g., a NASA URL that 404s, a fake astronaut quote, a fabricated statistic).
- **Missing uncertainty:** Verdict does not explicitly state the uncertainty (e.g., does not mention that LEO visibility is contested between astronauts, that primary NASA document was not retrieved, or that all claims are REPORTED/UNVERIFIABLE).
